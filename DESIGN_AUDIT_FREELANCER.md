# Yekis Freelancer Experience — Design & Frontend QA Audit (`DESIGN_AUDIT_FREELANCER.md`)

**Scope:** Freelancer Signup/Login, SMS Phone Verification, 5-Step Onboarding Tour & Checklist, Fayda eKYC & Tier 2 Verification, Freelancer Dashboard (`MarketplaceShadcnDashboard` & Overview), Public Profile & Portfolio Viewer, Profile & Portfolio Editor, Job Search/Browse, Proposals & Bidding, Gig Seller Studio (`My Gigs`), Contracts & Bilingual AACCSA Agreements, Milestones & Deliverables, Wallet / Earnings & Withdrawals (`Telebirr`, `CBE Birr`, `Safaricom M-Pesa`), Real-Time Messages & Notifications, 3-Stage Dispute Resolution, Settings, and Help/Support.  
**Shared Baseline Reused:** `DESIGN_AUDIT_ADMIN.md` unified status-to-tone semantics, tabular ETB currency formatters (`tabular-nums`), `ConfirmActionModal`, `EvidenceViewer` lightbox, and light/dark theme tokens (`apps/frontend/src/index.css` & `apps/frontend/src/styles/ops-console.css`).  
**Target Viewports Audited:** `375px` (Mobile — primary freelancer device class), `768px` (Tablet), `1280px` (Desktop), and `1920px` (Ultra-Wide) in **English (`en`)** and **Amharic (`am`)**.

## 2. End-to-End Freelancer Journey Friction Analysis

Walking the core Ethiopian freelancer journey from first visit to cash-out (`Signup` → `Phone OTP` → `Onboarding` → `Profile` → `KYC` → `Find Job` → `Send Proposal` → `Accept Contract` → `Deliver Work` → `Get Paid` → `Withdraw`) reveals **9 high-friction bottlenecks**:

1. **Step 1 — Signup & Phone OTP (`FullPlatformApp.tsx` → `PhoneVerification.tsx`)**:
   - *Friction*: On mobile (`<= 640px`), the absolute-positioned auth header bar (`FullPlatformApp.tsx:930–954`) can overlap the top of the registration panel. Once registered, `PhoneVerification.tsx` is 100% hardcoded English even if the user selected `አማርኛ (Amharic)` on the auth screen seconds earlier.
2. **Step 2 — Onboarding & Mobile Navigation Dead-End (`OnboardingTour.tsx` & `FullPlatformApp.tsx:1671–1682`)**:
   - *Friction*: A freelancer on a `375px` phone sees 8 crammed tabs in `.platform-mobile-nav` (`Overview`, `Jobs`, `My Gigs`, `Contracts`, `Disputes`, `Wallet`, `Verification`, `Messages`), while **Settings** and **My Profile** are completely unreachable because `.platform-sidebar` is hidden (`display: none` at `<= 640px`) and `.topbar-actions .user-menu` hides its label and only links to `profile`, leaving `Settings` (and Sign Out on mobile!) inaccessible without desktop width.
3. **Step 3 — Profile & Portfolio Setup (`ProfilePage.tsx` & `ProfileEditPage.tsx`)**:
   - *Friction*: Although `ProfilePage.tsx:257–280` renders `profile.portfolio` items if present, **`ProfileEditPage.tsx` has no UI section to add, edit, or upload portfolio items!** Freelancers are told to showcase their portfolio, and clients see a Portfolio section on profiles, but freelancers have no form controls in `ProfileEditPage.tsx` to add portfolio projects.
4. **Step 4 — Identity Verification (`BrowserVerification.tsx`)**:
   - *Friction*: The distinction between **Tier 1** (allows bidding on jobs) and **Tier 2** (required before withdrawing ETB earnings, and reduces ERCA withholding tax from 30% to 3% with verified TIN) is buried. File upload dropzones show raw filenames without file size validation feedback or preview thumbnails, and the entire flow lacks Amharic translations.
5. **Step 5 — Job Discovery & Bidding (`WorkflowWorkspace.tsx:660–746`)**:
   - *Friction*: When a freelancer types a bid amount (`Quote (ETB)`) in the proposal form, **there is no live fee or tax breakdown**. The freelancer cannot see whether `ETB 10,000` means they take home `ETB 10,000`, `ETB 9,000` (after 10% platform fee), or what withholding tax applies. Furthermore, `WorkflowWorkspace.tsx` uses `jobs` for both job browsing and proposal tracking without sub-tabs or jump anchors, forcing freelancers to scroll past all open marketplace briefs to find their own submitted proposals at the bottom of the page.
6. **Step 6 — Contract Review & Digital Signature (`WorkflowWorkspace.tsx:965–995`)**:
   - *Friction*: Clicking **"Preview Agreement (EN/AM)"** calls `window.open("", "_blank")` (`WorkflowWorkspace.tsx:969`). In sandboxed iframes and mobile in-app browsers, `window.open` is blocked, so the freelancer cannot read the contract before clicking **"Sign Contract"**—which itself executes immediately on a single click without a confirmation modal.
7. **Step 7 — Milestone Execution & Deliverable Submission (`WorkflowWorkspace.tsx:1010–1135`)**:
   - *Friction*: Milestone states render raw or semi-formatted strings (`ESCROW_HELD`, `WORK_IN_PROGRESS`, `SUBMITTED_FOR_REVIEW`). When a client has not yet funded a milestone (`PENDING_FUNDING`), the freelancer sees no warning badge explaining *"Wait for client to fund escrow before starting work"*. When submitting a deliverable, there is no confirmation summary reminding the freelancer that the 7-day client review timer (`auto-release`) starts upon submission.
8. **Step 8 — Getting Paid & Withdrawing to Telebirr / CBE / M-Pesa (`BrowserWallet.tsx:246–401`)**:
   - *Friction*: Clicking **"Request withdrawal"** immediately submits the payout mutation without a confirmation modal verifying the destination phone/account number (`+2519...` / `1000...`), rail (`Telebirr`, `CBE Birr`, `Safaricom M-Pesa`), and exact ETB amount. Additionally, inline `style={{ gridTemplateColumns: "repeat(3, minmax(0, 1fr))" }}` on `.payment-grid` (`BrowserWallet.tsx:442,614`) overrides mobile CSS media queries, crushing the 3 payment rail cards on `375px` screens.
9. **Step 9 — Disputes & Client Messaging (`ChatWorkspace.tsx` & `WorkflowWorkspace.tsx:1137–1238`)**:
   - *Friction*: In `WorkflowWorkspace.tsx:1155`, dispute stages display raw backend enum strings (`STAGE_1_NEGOTIATION`, `STAGE_2_EVIDENCE`, `STAGE_3_MEDIATION`) instead of human-friendly bilingual stage badges with clear next-step instructions.
