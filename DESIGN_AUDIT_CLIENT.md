## 2. End-to-End Client Journey Friction Analysis

Walking the core Ethiopian client hiring journey from first registration to contract completion (`Signup` → `Post Job` → `Review Proposals` → `Hire` → `Create Contract` → `Fund Escrow` → `Receive Work` → `Approve` → `Pay` → `Receipt / Invoice`, plus the `Dispute` path) reveals **10 critical friction bottlenecks**:

1. **Step 1 — Client Signup & Role Clarity (`FullPlatformApp.tsx`)**:
   - *Friction*: When registering as a `CLIENT` (or `BOTH`), the onboarding dialog introduces general marketplace features without emphasizing **Client Escrow Protection** (100% money-back guarantee if work is not delivered, AACCSA mediation rules, and how funds are held in trust until explicit client sign-off).
2. **Step 2 — Post a Job Form Lacks Financial Guidance & Milestone Breakdown (`FullPlatformApp.tsx:1912–2010`)**:
   - *Friction*: The job creation form asks for `Minimum Budget (ETB)` and `Maximum Budget (ETB)` as raw numbers. It does not provide an inline **Escrow Deposit Estimation** (e.g., explaining that clients will only deposit funds when hiring, not at posting), gives no breakdown of applicable platform fees or VAT, has no milestone breakdown schedule, and lacks a "Preview Brief" step before publishing.
3. **Step 3 — Proposal Pipeline Lacks Freelancer Verification & Profile Access (`WorkflowWorkspace.tsx:215–334`)**:
   - *Friction*: In the client's `Published Hiring Briefs & Proposal Pipeline`, applicant proposals only show `proposal.freelancer?.email` and plain text cover letters. **There is no verification badge (Fayda Tier 1/2), no star rating, no completed jobs count, and no link or button to open the freelancer's public profile modal!** A client cannot inspect a freelancer's previous portfolio work, bio, or reviews before deciding whether to hire them.
4. **Step 4 — Unconfirmed Instant Proposal Acceptance (`WorkflowWorkspace.tsx:280–300`)**:
   - *Friction*: Clicking **"Accept"** on a proposal immediately invokes `api.decideProposal(proposal.id, "ACCEPTED")`, binding the client to a legal contract without showing a summary confirmation modal with the quoted ETB amount, estimated delivery timeline, milestone breakdown, and payment obligations.
5. **Step 5 — Instant Escrow Funding Without Confirmation or Balance Verification (`WorkflowWorkspace.tsx:1008–1045`)**:
   - *Friction*: Clicking **"Fund Escrow"** on a milestone immediately executes `api.fundMilestoneFromWallet(milestone.id)` in a single click. There is no confirmation dialog displaying the milestone amount, counterparty freelancer, fee breakdown, or current wallet balance. If the client has insufficient balance, it throws a generic error instead of guiding them to top up their wallet.
6. **Step 6 — Instant Deliverable Approval Without QA Confirmation (`WorkflowWorkspace.tsx:1085–1121`)**:
   - *Friction*: Clicking **"Accept & Release Funds"** on a submitted milestone immediately executes `api.acceptMilestone(milestone.id)`. Releasing funds is irreversible, yet there is no confirmation modal asking the client to verify that deliverables satisfy acceptance criteria, nor does it prompt for an optional freelancer rating and review.
7. **Step 7 — Absence of Printable Payment Receipts / Invoices (`BrowserWallet.tsx:788–880`)**:
   - *Friction*: When an Ethiopian business or individual client deposits 25,000 ETB or releases 15,000 ETB for software development, there is **no printable ETB receipt or tax invoice**. The transaction row shows only raw text without an option to view or print an official payment voucher containing the reference number, counterparty, VAT breakdown, and platform details.
8. **Step 8 — Talent Discovery in Gig Marketplace Lacks Safe Checkout & Trust Badges (`GigMarketplace.tsx`)**:
   - *Friction*: In the Gig Marketplace, gigs display basic cards with no seller Fayda KYC verification badges and unformatted rating numbers. Clicking **"Order & Pay"** instantly creates AND funds the order (`api.createGigOrder` + `api.fundGigOrder`) without a confirmation modal, without escrow fee breakdown, and without checking wallet balance.
9. **Step 9 — Unclear Dispute Initiation & Stage Explanation (`WorkflowWorkspace.tsx:1137–1260`)**:
   - *Friction*: When a client clicks "Dispute" on an escrow milestone, the dropdown asks for a category and textarea, but does not explain that opening a dispute freezes escrow and initiates **Stage 1: Direct 72-Hour Negotiation**, followed by **Stage 2: Evidence Submission**, and finally **Stage 3: Neutral AACCSA Mediation**.
10. **Step 10 — Pervasive Localization Gap across Client Flows (`WorkflowWorkspace.tsx`, `FullPlatformApp.tsx`, `GigMarketplace.tsx`, `BrowserWallet.tsx`)**:
    - *Friction*: All hiring pipeline cards, job posting inputs, milestone escrow buttons, and gig checkout screens contain 100% hardcoded English strings. Ethiopian clients working in Amharic (`am`) see English forms throughout their hiring and payment journeys.

## 4. Cross-Role Inconsistencies vs. Admin & Freelancer Audits

1. **Unconfirmed Financial Mutations**:
   - `DESIGN_AUDIT_ADMIN.md` (AUD-01, AUD-02) and `DESIGN_AUDIT_FREELANCER.md` (FR-04) strictly enforced `ConfirmActionModal` on all money-moving and compliance mutations. On the Client side, milestone escrow funding, milestone acceptance, gig checkout, and proposal hiring were all executing on single unconfirmed clicks.
2. **Tabular ETB Formatting**:
   - The Admin audit unified all numeric amounts to `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` and `formatEtbMajor`. The Freelancer audit adopted this. However, Client screens still used a mix of `money()`, `formatBirr()`, and raw `.toLocaleString()`.
3. **Status Badges & Semantics**:
   - Admin and Freelancer standardized on text-first status pills with unified color tokens (`p-green`, `p-sky`, `p-amber`, `p-crimson`, `p-violet`). Client proposal lists and milestone cards still used ad-hoc inline classes (`emerald`, `crimson`, `amber`) and raw backend enum strings (`PAYMENT_PENDING`, `SUBMITTED_FOR_REVIEW`).
4. **Talent Discovery Disconnect**:
   - Freelancers edit their profile and portfolio (`ProfilePage.tsx`), but clients reviewing incoming proposals or browsing the gig marketplace had no direct link or modal to inspect the candidate's full profile, portfolio items, or verification badges.
5. **Printable Invoices & Receipts**:
   - Neither the admin nor freelancer side addressed official client fiscal receipts. For Ethiopian businesses hiring on Yekis, having a printable, verifiable ETB receipt with VAT/platform fee breakdown and AACCSA contract reference is essential for accounting and trust.

## 5. What Could Not Be Verified

1. **Physical Payment Rail Push Notifications**:
   - Sandboxed test environment cannot verify live push prompts sent to physical Telebirr mobile apps or CBE Birr USSD menus. Verified via simulated mock checkout and manual transaction receipt attachment workflows.
2. **Physical Mobile Printer Spooling**:
   - Native printer spooling across diverse mobile browsers (iOS Safari AirPrint vs Android Chrome Print) cannot be physically printed in headless preview; verified via standard `@media print` CSS rules and clean printable modal layouts.
