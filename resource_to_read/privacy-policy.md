## 1. ENGLISH VERSION

### 1.1 Introduction & Legal Framework
This Privacy Policy outlines how Ethiopian Freelance Marketplace PLC ("we," "our," or "the Platform") collects, processes, stores, and protects personal data of freelancers and clients ("you" or "User") under:
1. **Personal Data Protection Proclamation No. 1321/2024 (PDPP)**
2. **Prevention and Suppression of Money Laundering and Financing of Terrorism Proclamation No. 1387/2025 (AML Proclamation)**
3. **Electronic Transactions Proclamation No. 1205/2020**
4. **National Bank of Ethiopia (NBE) Directive FIS/04/2021**

### 1.2 Granular User Consent
By registering on the Platform and using our services, you are asked to provide granular, unbundled consent for different processing activities:
*   **KYC Identity Verification [Mandatory]:** Consent to submit your national identity details and Captured Biometrics to the National ID Program (NIDP) via the VeriFayda 2 API for identity verification and fraud prevention under Proclamation No. 1284/2023.
*   **Financial Data Processing [Mandatory]:** Consent to process bank details, wallet IDs, and transaction history for escrow holding, fee calculation, and payouts via Chapa and Telebirr.
*   **Platform Notifications [Optional]:** Consent to receive transactional status alerts and updates via SMS or Telegram.
*   **Marketing Use of Profile [Optional]:** Consent to display your public profile, ratings, and portfolios for promotional purposes.
*   **Anonymized Analytics [Optional]:** Consent to share aggregated, non-identifiable usage statistics for product improvement.

### 1.3 Personal Data We Process & Purposes
To run a secure, contract-backed marketplace with integrated escrow, we collect:
*   **Identity Data (Tiers 1 & 2):** Name, phone number, address, email, government-issued ID (Fayda ID preferred), and captured biometrics (processed in memory, never stored on our servers).
*   **Financial & Tax Data (Tier 2):** Bank account number, mobile money wallet ID (Telebirr/CBE Birr), Tax Identification Number (TIN), and platform transaction logs.
*   **Transaction & Messaging Data:** Project descriptions, digital signatures of Master Service Agreements (MSAs), milestone deliverables (complete with SHA-256 hashes), in-app chat histories, and dispute mediation files.

### 1.4 Financial Payouts & Statutory Taxes (ERCA Compliance)
Your payouts are subject to automated tax and commission splits:
*   **Platform Fee:** 10% total fee (split 5% from client's deposit and 5% deducted from the freelancer's payout).
*   **Withholding Tax:** For payouts of 10,000 ETB or more, the platform automatically deducts **3% withholding tax** if a verified TIN is provided, or a **30% hard-block withholding tax** if no TIN is on file.
*   **VAT:** 15% VAT is calculated on the platform's commission revenue and tracked separately as a platform liability (never deducted from the freelancer's payout).

### 1.5 Strict Data Localization & Sovereignty (PDPP Art. 22)
*   **Primary Storage:** All raw personal data, including KYC documents, transaction logs, contracts, messages, and dispute records, is hosted strictly on domestic cloud servers within Ethiopia (**Ethio Telecom Cloud** or **Safaricom Ethiopia Cloud**).
*   **Disaster Recovery Backups:** Encrypted backups transmitted to international cloud systems (AWS/Azure/GCP) are protected by ECA-approved Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and AES-256 encryption at rest.

### 1.6 Dispute Resolution & Message Confidentiality
*   **Permitted Disclosure:** Under Proclamation No. 1205/2020, electronic messages are confidential. However, to resolve escrow quality disputes, the ToS and MSA establish a "permitted disclosure" environment where our trained mediators can access in-app message logs.
*   **Off-Platform Communications:** All communications must remain on-platform. Messaging on WhatsApp or Telegram Direct Messages is explicitly excluded from dispute evidence.
*   **Arbitration:** If mediation fails, disputes escalate to binding arbitration in Addis Ababa under **AACCSA Rules** and Proclamation No. 1237/2021, with an **express waiver of cassation-bench review** for fundamental errors of law under Art. 49(2).

### 1.7 Data Retention & The Deletion Conflict (AML vs. PDPP)
*   **10-Year Mandatory Hold:** Under Proclamation No. 1387/2025, the platform is legally required to retain all customer identification (KYC) records, transaction history, STR filings, and dispute archives for a **minimum of 10 years** after the business relationship ends.
*   **Logical Segregation & Deletion:** If you invoke your **Right to Erasure**, the system will immediately soft-delete and purge all non-AML data (marketing preferences, profile views, and chat logs outside active disputes). Retained AML and financial ledger logs are logically isolated and encrypted on WORM (Write Once Read Many) storage, accessible only to compliance auditors.

### 1.8 Your Rights & Complaints
Under Chapter 3 of the PDPP, you have the right to access (via a "Download My Data" button yielding JSON/CSV inside 30 days), rectify, restrict, and object to processing. You may file compliance complaints directly with the **Ethiopian Communications Authority (ECA)**.

*   **የአደጋ ጊዜ መጠባበቂያ (Backups)፦** ለአደጋ ጊዜ መጠባበቂያነት ወደ አለምአቀፍ ክላውድ (AWS/Azure/GCP) የሚላኩ መረጃዎች በAES-256 ኢንክሪፕሽን የተቆለፉ መሆን አለባቸው፤ እንዲሁም በኢትዮጵያ ኮሙኒኬሽን ባለስልጣን (ECA) የጸደቁ መደበኛ የውል ስምምነቶች (SCCs) ሊኖራቸው ይገባል።

### 2.6 የግጭት አፈታት እና የመልዕክት ሚስጥራዊነት
*   **የተፈቀደ ይፋዊነት፦** በአዋጅ ቁጥር 1205/2012 መሰረት የኤሌክትሮኒክስ መልዕክቶች ሚስጥራዊነታቸው የተጠበቀ ነው። ነገር ግን በአደራ ሂሳብ (Escrow) የጥራት እና የስራ ክርክር ሲነሳ የፕላትፎርሙ አደራዳሪዎች የውስጥ መልዕክቶችን መመልከት እንዲችሉ በውል ስምምነቱ (ToS/MSA) ውስጥ ልዩ ፈቃድ ተጠቃሚው ይሰጣል።
*   **ከፕላትፎርም ውጭ የሚደረጉ ግንኙነቶች፦** ሁሉም የስራ ግንኙነቶች በፕላትፎርሙ የውስጥ መልዕክት መለዋወጫ ብቻ መደረግ አለባቸው። በዋትስአፕ (WhatsApp) ወይም በቴሌግራም ቀጥታ መልዕክት (Telegram DM) የተደረጉ ግንኙነቶች በምስክርነት በፍጹም ተቀባይነት የላቸውም።
*   **ግልገሌ ዳኝነት (Arbitration)፦** የፕላትፎርሙ እምነት ማጣት አፈታት ካልተሳካ ክርክሩ በአዲስ አበባ በሚገኘው **የአዲስ አበባ ንግድና ዘርፍ ማኅበራት ምክር ቤት የዕርቅና ዳኝነት ማዕከል (AACCSA)** በአዋጅ ቁጥር 1237/2013 መሰረት የሚታይ ሲሆን፤ በአንቀጽ 49(2) መሰረት የሰበር ሰሚ ችሎት ግምገማ መብት በውል በግልጽ ይተዋል።

### 2.7 የመረጃ ማከማቻ ጊዜ እና የስረዛ መብት (የአዋጆች ቅራኔ)
*   **የ10 ዓመት የግዴታ ማከማቻ ጊዜ፦** በአዋጅ ቁጥር 1387/2017 መሰረት ፕላትፎርሙ ሁሉንም የማንነት ማረጋገጫ (KYC) መረጃዎች፣ የግብይት ታሪኮችን፣ የSTR ሪፖርቶችን እና የክርክር መዝገቦችን የስራ ግንኙነቱ ካበቃበት ቀን ጀምሮ ለ**10 ዓመታት** በህግ የማቆየት ግዴታ አለበት።
*   **የመረጃ አያያዝ እና ስረዛ፦** ተጠቃሚው የመሰረዝ መብቱን (Right to Erasure) ሲጠይቅ፣ ፕላትፎርሙ ከህግ ውጭ የሆኑ መረጃዎችን (የማስታወቂያ ምርጫዎች፣ የፕሮፋይል እይታዎች እና ከክርክር ውጭ የሆኑ መልዕክቶችን) ወዲያውኑ ይሰርዛል። ነገር ግን የKYC እና የፋይናንስ መረጃዎች በWORM (Write Once Read Many) ማከማቻ ውስጥ ተለይተው ለ10 ዓመታት በኢንክሪፕሽን ተቆልፈው ይቀመጣሉ።

### 2.8 የእርስዎ መብቶች እና ቅሬታ ማቅረቢያ
በግል መረጃ ጥበቃ አዋጅ ምዕራፍ 3 መሰረት የመረጃዎን ዝርዝር የማግኘት (በJSON/CSV ፎርማት በ30 ቀናት ውስጥ)፣ የማስተካከል፣ የማገድ እና የመቃወም መብት አለዎት። ቅሬታዎን ለ**ኢትዮጵያ ኮሙኒኬሽን ባለስልጣን (ECA)** ማቅረብ ይችላሉ።

## 3. AFAN OROMO VERSION (IMAAMMATA EEGUMSA ODDEEFFANNOO DHUUNFAA)

### 3.1 Seensa fi Daangaa Seeraa
Imaammati Eegumsa Odeeffannoo Dhuunfaa kun hojjettoota dhuunfaa (freelancers) fi maamiltoota (isin ykn "Fayyadamaa") odeeffannoo dhuunfaa isaanii akkamitti akka sassaabnu, ijaarru, kuusnu fi eegnu kan ibsudha. Kunis seera Itoophiyaa armaan gadii irratti hundaa'uun raawwatama:
1. **Labsii Eegumsa Odeeffannoo Dhuunfaa Lak. 1321/2016 (PDPP)**
2. **Labsii Haqama Maallaqaa fi Deeggarsa Shurubbaa Shororkeessummaa Ittisuu fi To'achuu Lak. 1387/2017 (AML Proclamation)**
3. **Labsii Giddu-gala Daldala Elektrooniksii Lak. 1205/2012**
4. **Qajeelfama Baankii Biyyoolessa Itoophiyaa Lak. FIS/04/2021**

### 3.2 Hayyama Fayyadamaa (Consent)
Pilaatfoormii keenya irratti yeroo galmooftan dhimmoota adda addaaf hayyama ifa ta'e akka kennitan gaafatama:
*   **Mirkaneessa Eenyummaa (KYC Verification) [Dirqama]:** Akka Labsii Lak. 1284/2015 eenyummaa keessan mirkaneessuuf fi fraud ittisuuf odeeffannoo eenyummaa keessan fi fakkii nagaan keessan VeriFayda 2 API fayyadamuun gara Piroogiraamii Eenyummaa Biyyoolessaatti (NIDP) ergamuuf hayyamuu.
*   **Maallaqa fi Hojii To'achuu [Dirqama]:** Chapa fi Telebirr fayyadamuun kuusaa wabii (Escrow) keessatti maallaqa qabuuf, kaffaltii piilaatfoormii herreguuf fi kaffaltii raawwachuuf odeeffannoo herrega baankii, kaffaltii moobaayilaa fi seenaa daldalaa fayyadamuu hayyamuu.
*   **Beeksisa Pilaatfoormii [Filannoo]:** Waa'ee pirojektoota fi kaffaltii keessanii odeeffannoo gaggabaaboo SMS ykn Telegram dhaan argachuuf hayyamuu.
*   **Beeksisa Profaayilii [Filannoo]:** Profaayilii, sadarkaa gamaaggama fayyadamaa fi hojiiwwan hojjettan beeksisaaf akka fayyadamnu hayyamuu.
*   **Odeeffannoo Walii-galaa [Filannoo]:** Pilaatfoormii keenya fooyyessuuf odeeffannoo eenyummaa hin ibsine qorannoo istaatistiikiif akka fayyadamnu hayyamuu.

### 3.3 Odeeffannoo Dhuunfaa Sassaabnu fi Kaayyoo Isaanii
Hojii amansiisaa fi kuusaa wabii (Escrow) irratti hundaa'e uumuuf odeeffannoo armaan gadii ni sassaabna:
*   **Odeeffannoo Eenyummaa (Sadarkaa 1 fi 2):** Maqaa, lakk. bilbilaa, teessoo, email, eenyummaa mootummaa (Fayda ID ni filatama) fi fakkii fuulaa (milloofna yeroof qofa kan adeemsifamu, kuusaa piilaatfoormii irratti hin kuusamu).
*   **Odeeffannoo Maallaqaa fi Gibiraa (Sadarkaa 2):** Lakk. herrega baankii, kaffaltii moobaayilaa (Telebirr/CBE Birr), lakk. eenyummaa kaffalaa gibiraa (TIN) fi galmeewwan daldalaa.
*   **Odeeffannoo Hojii fi Ergaa:** Ibsa pirojektootaa, mallattoo elektrooniksii waliigaltee (Master Service Agreement), hojii milestone fi koodii SHA-256 hash, ergaawwan piilaatfoormii keessaa fi galmeewwan wal-dhabdee.

### 3.4 Kaffaltii fi Gibira Seeraa (ERCA)
Kaffaltiin hojjettoota dhuunfaa gibira seeraa fi kaffaltii piilaatfoormii irratti hundaa'uun herregama:
*   **Kaffaltii Pilaatfoormii (Commission):** Waliigala 10% (5% kaffalaa irraa dabalataan kaffalama, 5% hojjetaa dhuunfaa irraa ni hir'ifama).
*   **Gibira Hir'ifamu (Withholding Tax):** Pirojektii kaffaltiin isaa 10,000 ETB ykn isaa ol ta'eef: lakk. eenyummaa kaffalaa gibiraa (TIN) qabaannaan **3% withholding tax**, TIN yoo hin qabaanne garuu **30% withholding tax** kaffaltii irraa hir'ifamee gara Ministeera Galiiwwaniitti ergama.
*   **VAT:** Komishinii pilaatfoormii irraa 15% VAT herregama, kunis eeyyama pilaatfoormii keenyati malee kaffaltii hojjetaa irraa hin hir'ifamu.

### 3.5 Eegumsa Odeeffannoo Biyyoolessaa (Sovereignty - PDPP Art. 22)
*   **Kuusaa Odeeffannoo Guddaa:** Odeeffannoon dhuunfaa hundi, ragaaleen KYC, seenaan kaffaltii, waliigalteewwan, ergaawwan fi ragaaleen falmii Itoophiyaa keessatti kuusaa cloud biyyoolessaa (**Ethio Telecom Cloud** ykn **Safaricom Ethiopia Cloud**) irratti qofa kuusamuu qabu.
*   **Kofii Backup (Kofii dabalataa):** Backup gara biyya alaa (AWS/Azure/GCP) deeggaruuf ergamu kamiyyuu AES-256 encryption dhaan hidhamuu qaba, ragaalee eeyyama ECA fi mormii dhabuuf Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) qabaachuu qaba.

### 3.6 Hiika Wal-dhabdee fi Ictisummaa Ergaa
*   **Hayyama Ifaa:** Labsii Lak. 1205/2012 tiin ergaawwan elektrooniksii dhuunfaa eegumsa qabu. Garuu falmiin yeroo uumamu, ragaalee mirkaneessuuf to'attoonni keenya akka ergaa keessan ilaalan waliigaltee keessatti hayyamni ni kennama.
*   **Gargarsa Alaa:** Ergaawwan hundi piilaatfoormii keessaan qofa raawwatamuu qabu. WhatsApp ykn Telegram Direct Message ragaaf fudhatama hin qaban.
*   **Arbitration (Jaarsummaa):** Falmiin pilaatfoormii keenyaan hin furamne, gara **AACCSA** tti geeffamee Labsii Lak. 1237/2013 tiin kan ilaalamu yoo ta'u, seera kassaasiin falmuu akka dhiisan irratti waliigalamu qaba (Art. 49(2)).

### 3.7 Yeroo Kuusaa fi Mirga Haqamuu (AML vs. PDPP)
*   **Dirqama Waggaa 10 Kuusuu:** Akka Labsii Lak. 1387/2017 tti, pilaatfoormiin keenya ragaaleen KYC, daldalaa, beeksisa STR fi galmee wal-dhabdee hunda erga waliigalteen raawwatamee booda **waggaa 10** kuusuuf dirqama qaba.
*   **Mirga Haqamuu Fayyadamaa:** Fayyadamaan eenyummaa isaa akka haqamu yoo gaafate, ragaalee beeksisaa fi ergaawwan dhuunfaa ni haana. Garuu KYC fi galmeen kaffaltii ragaa AML tiif waggaa 10f kuusaa WORM (Write Once Read Many) keessatti eegamee kuusama.

### 3.8 Mirga Keessan fi Kofii Mormii
Labsii Eegumsa Odeeffannoo Dhuunfaa Keeyyata 3 ragaa dhuunfaa keessan gaafachuu (foormii JSON/CSV guyyaa 30 keessatti), sirreessuu, dhorkuu fi mormuuf mirga qabdu. Mormii keessan kallattiin **ECA** f dhiyeessuu ni dandeessu.

## 4. TIGRIGNA VERSION (ፖሊሲ ዕቃበ ውልቃዊ መረዳእታ)

### 4.1 መእተዊን ሕጋዊ መሰረትን
እዚ ፖሊሲ ዕቃበ ውልቃዊ መረዳእታ ናይ ኢትዮጵያ ፍሪላንስ ማርኬትፕሌስ ኃ/የተ/የግ/ማኅበር (ካብዚ ንድሓር "ንሕና" ወይ "ፕላትፎርም" እናተባህለ ዝፅዋዕ) ናይ ዓማዊልን ፍሪላንሰራትን (ካብዚ ንድሓር "ንስኻትኩም" ወይ "ተጠቃሚ" እናተባሃልኩም እትፅውዑ) ውልቃዊ መረዳእታ ብኸመይ ከምዝእክብ፣ ከምዘሰናድእ፣ ከምዝዕቅብን ከምዝከላኸልን ዝገልጽ እዩ። እዚ ዝስዕቡ ናይ ኢፌዴሪ ሕግታት መሰረት ገይሩ ዝፍፀም እዩ፡
1. **ኣዋጅ ዕቃበ ውልቃዊ መረዳእታ ቁፅሪ 1321/2016 (PDPP)**
2. **ኣዋጅ ምክልኻልን ምቁፅጻርን ሕጋዊ መሲልካ ምቕራብ እቶት ገበንን ምውፋር ገንዘብ ራዕድን ቁፅሪ 1387/2017 (AML Proclamation)**
3. **ኣዋጅ ኤሌክትሮኒክስ መገበያይ ቁፅሪ 1205/2012**
4. **መምርሒ ሃገራዊ ባንኪ ኢትዮጵያ ቁፅሪ FIS/04/2021**

### 4.2 ዝርዝር ፍቓድ ተጠቃሚ (Consent)
ኣብዚ ፕላትፎርም ክትምዝገቡን ኣገልግሎትና ክትጥቀሙን ከለኹም ንዝተፈላለዩ ናይ መረዳእታ ምስንዳእ ተግባራት ንጹርን ዘይተሓዋወሰን ፍቓድ ክትህቡ ትሕተቱ ኢኹም፡
*   **ምርግጋፅ መንነት (KYC Verification) [ግዴታ]፦** ብመሰረት ኣዋጅ ቁፅሪ 1284/2015 መንነትኩም ንምርግጋፅን ምትላል ንምክልኻልን ናይ ሃገራዊ መታወቂያ መረዳእታኹምን ባዮሜትሪክስ (Biometrics) ምስልኹምን ብVeriFayda 2 API ኣቢልኩም ናብ ሃገራዊ መታወቂያ ፕሮግራም (NIDP) ንምልኣኽ ፍቓድ ምሃብ።
*   **ምስንዳእ መረዳእታ ፋይናንስ [ግዴታ]፦** ብChapa ን Telebirr ን ኣቢሉ ብውሑስ ሕሳብ (Escrow) ገንዘብ ንምሓዝ፣ ናይ ኣገልግሎት ክፍሊት ንምሕሳብን ክፍሊታት ንምፍጻምን ናይ ባንኪ ሕሳብ ቑፅሪኹም፣ ናይ ሞባይል ገንዘብ መለለዪኹምን ናይ ንግዲ ታሪኽኩምን ንምስንዳእ ፍቓድ ምሃብ።
*   **ናይ ፕላትፎርም ምልክታታት [ከተፍልዮ]፦** ብዛዕባ ፕሮጀክትታትኩምን ክፍሊትኩምን ኩነታት ብፅሑፍ መልእኽቲ (SMS) ወይ ብቴሌግራም ቅልጡፍ ምልክታ ንምቕባል ፍቓድ ምሃብ።
*   **ፕሮፋይል ንምፍላጥ [ከተፍልዮ]፦** ናትኩም ናይ ህዝቢ ፕሮፋይል፣ ገምጋም ተጠቃሚ ደረጃን ዘሰናደእኹምዎ ናይ ፖርትፎሊዮ ስራሕን ንምፍላጥ ክንጥቀመሉ ፍቓድ ምሃብ።
*   **መንነት ዘይገልፅ ስታቲስቲክስ (Anonymized Analytics) [ከተፍልዮ]፦** ነዚ ፕላትፎርም ንምምሕያሽ መንነት ዘይገልፁ ሓፈሻዊ መረዳእታታት ንመፅናዕቲ ስታቲስቲክስ ንምክፋል ፍቓድ ምሃብ።

### 4.3 እነሰናድኦ ውልቃዊ መረዳእታን ዕላምኡን
ውሑስን ኣብ ውሑስ ሕሳብ (Escrow) ዝተመስረተን ናይ ስራሕ ምትእስሳር ንምፍጣር እዞም ዝስዕቡ መረዳእታታት ንእክብ፡
*   **መረዳእታ መንነት (ደረጃ 1 ን 2)፦** ስም፣ ቑፅሪ ስልኪ፣ ኣድራሻ፣ ኢሜል፣ ብመንግስቲ ዝተውሃበ መታወቂያ (ፋይዳ መታወቂያ ይምረፅ)ን ተንቀሳቓሲ ምስሊ ገፅ (ኣብ ግዝያዊ ሜሞሪ ጥራሕ ዝስነድ፣ ኣብ ሰርቨር ፕላትፎርም ብፍፁም ዘይዕቀብ)።
*   **መረዳእታ ፋይናንስን ታክስን (ደረጃ 2)፦** ቑፅሪ ሕሳብ ባንኪ፣ ናይ ሞባይል ገንዘብ መለለዪ (Telebirr/CBE Birr)፣ ናይ ግብሪ ከፋሊ መለለዪ ቁፅሪ (TIN) ን ናይ ንግዲ መዝገብን።
*   **መረዳእታ ንግድን መልእኽትን፦** ናይ ፕሮጀክት መግለጺታት፣ ናይ ኤሌክትሮኒክስ ፊርማታት ዋና ውዕል ኣገልግሎት (Master Service Agreement)፣ ናይ milestone ስራሕትን መለለዪታት SHA-256 hash ን፣ ኣብ ውሽጢ ፕላትፎርም ዝተገብሩ ናይ መልእኽቲ ልውውጥን ናይ ምስሕሓብ መዝገብን።

### 4.4 ክፍሊት ፋይናንስን ሕጋዊ ግብርን (ERCA)
ናይ ፍሪላንሰራት ክፍሊት ብመሰረት እዞም ዝስዕቡ ሕጋዊ ታክስን ኮሚሽንን ብኣውቶማቲክ ተቐናሽ ይኸውን፡
*   **ክፍሊት ኣገልግሎት ፕላትፎርም (Commission)፦** ብሓፈሻ 10% (5% ካብ ቆጻሪ ተወሲኹ ዝኽፈል፣ 5% ድማ ካብ ናይ ፍሪላንሰር ክፍሊት ተቐናሽ ዝግበር)።
*   **ተቐናሽ ግብሪ (Withholding Tax)፦** ንሓደ ፕሮጀክት ዝኽፈል ክፍሊት 10,000 ናይ ኢትዮጵያ ብር ወይ ከዚያ ንላዕሊ እንተኾይኑ፡ ዝተረጋገፀ ናይ ግብሪ ከፋሊ መለለዪ ቁፅሪ (TIN) እንተሃሊዩ **3% ተቐናሽ ግብሪ**፣ ናይ TIN ቁፅሪ እንተዘይሃሊዩ ግን **30% ተቐናሽ ግብሪ** ብኣውቶማቲክ ተቐናሽ ተገይሩ ናብ ሚኒስቴር እቶታት ይእቶ።
*   **ተወሳኺ እሴት ታክስ (VAT)፦** ፕላትፎርም ኣብ ዝረኽቦ ናይ ኮሚሽን እቶት 15% VAT ዝሕሰብ ኮይኑ፣ እዚ ታክስ ናይ ፕላትፎርም ዕዳ እምበር ካብ ናይ ፍሪላንሰር ክፍሊት ብፍፁም ተቐናሽ ኣይኸውንን።

### 4.5 ፅኑዕ ውሽጣዊ መረዳእታ ዕቃበ (ሉዓላዊነት - PDPP አንቀጽ 22)
*   **ቀንዲ መረዳእታ መዕቀቢ፦** ኩሎም ናይ ተጠቀምቲ ውልቃዊ መረዳእታታት፣ ናይ KYC ሰነዳት፣ ናይ ክፍሊት ታሪኽ፣ ውዕላት፣ መልእኽትታትን ናይ ክርክር መዝገብን ኣብ ኢትዮጵያ ውሽጢ ኣብ ዝርከቡ ሰርቨራት (**ኢትዮ ቴሌኮም ክላውድ** ወይ **ሳፋሪኮም ኢትዮጵያ ክላውድ**) ጥራሕ ክዕቀቡ ኣለዎም።
*   **ናይ ሓደጋ ግዜ መዕቀቢ (Backups)፦** ንሓደጋ ግዜ መዕቀቢ ናብ ኣህጉራዊ ክላውድ (AWS/Azure/GCP) ዝለኣኹ መረዳእታታት ብAES-256 ኢንክሪፕሽን ዝተቖለፉ ክኾኑ ኣለዎም፤ ከምኡ እውን ብECA ዝጸደቑ መደበኛ ውዕላት (SCCs) ክህልውዎም ይግባእ።

### 4.6 መፍትሒ ምስሕሓብን ምስጢራውነት መልእኽትን
*   **ፍቑድ ግልፅነት፦** ብመሰረት ኣዋጅ ቁፅሪ 1205/2012 ናይ ኤሌክትሮኒክስ መልእኽትታት ምስጢራውነት ዝተሓለወ እዩ። ግናኸ ምስሕሓብ ክለዓል ከሎ፣ መረዳእታ ንምርግጋፅ ናይ ፕላትፎርም ዓረቅቲ ናይ ውሽጢ መልእኽቲ ክርእዩ ከምዝኽእሉ ኣብ ውዕል ስምምዕ (ToS/MSA) ፍሉይ ፍቓድ ተጠቃሚ ይህብ።
*   **ካብ ፕላትፎርም ወፃኢ ዝግበሩ ርክባት፦** ኩሎም ናይ ስራሕ ርክባት ኣብ ውሽጢ ፕላትፎርም ጥራሕ ክግበሩ ኣለዎም። ብWhatsApp ወይ ብTelegram Direct Message ዝተገብሩ ርክባት ንምስክርነት በፍፁም ተቐባልነት የብሎምን።
*   **ሽምግልና (Arbitration)፦** ናይ ፕላትፎርም ዕርቂ እንተዘይተሳኺዑ፣ እቲ ክርክር ኣብ ኣዲስ ኣበባ ኣብ ዝርከብ **ቤት ምኽሪ ንግድን ዘርፍ ማሕበራትን ኣዲስ ኣበባ (AACCSA)** ብመሰረት ኣዋጅ ቁፅሪ 1237/2013 ዝርአ ኮይኑ፤ ብመሰረት ዓንቀፅ 49(2) ናይ ሰበር ሰሚ ችሎት ግምገማ መሰል ብውዕል ብንጹር ይሕደግ።

### 4.7 ናይ መረዳእታ ዕቃበ ግዜን መሰል ምስራዝን (AML vs. PDPP)
*   **ናይ 10 ዓመት ግዴታ ዕቃበ ግዜ፦** ብመሰረት ኣዋጅ ቁፅሪ 1387/2017፣ ፕላትፎርም ኩሎም ናይ KYC መረዳእታታት፣ ናይ ክፍሊት ታሪኽ፣ ናይ STR ሪፖርትታት እንተላይ ናይ ክርክር መዝገብ ድሕሪ ውዕል ምብቃዕ ን**10 ዓመታት** ክዕቅብ ሕጋዊ ግዴታ ኣለዎ።
*   **መሰል ምስራዝ ተጠቃሚ፦** ተጠቃሚ መሰል ምስራዝ (Right to Erasure) ክጥቀም ከሎ፣ ምስ AML ዘይተተሓሓዙ መረዳእታታት (ምርጫ ምልክታታት፣ ናይ ፕሮፋይል ምርኣይን ካብ ክርክር ወፃኢ ዝኾኑ መልእኽትታትን) ወዲያውኑ ክስረዙ እዮም። ግናኸ ናይ KYC ን ፋይናንስን መረዳእታታት ኣብ WORM (Write Once Read Many) መዕቀቢ ተፈልዮም ን10 ዓመት ይዕቀቡ።

### 4.8 ናይ ንስኻትኩም መሰላትን መማረፂ ጥርዓንን
ብመሰረት ኣዋጅ ዕቃበ ውልቃዊ መረዳእታ ምዕራፍ 3 መረዳእታኹም ናይ ምርካብ (ብJSON/CSV ፎርማት ኣብ 30 መዓልቲ ውሽጢ)፣ የማስተካከል፣ ናይ ምእጋድን ናይ ምቅዋምን መሰል ኣለኩም። ጥርዓንኩም ብቐጥታ ናብ **ECA** ከውርዱ ትኽእሉ ኢኹም።
