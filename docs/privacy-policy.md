# Privacy Policy

This privacy policy outlines how the Ethiopian Freelance Marketplace collects, uses, stores, and protects user data in accordance with Ethiopian data protection regulations.

## Data Collection
- Personal identification information (name, email, phone, KYC documents).
- Financial information for escrow and payouts.
- Usage analytics for platform improvement.

## Use of Data
- To verify identity and comply with AML/KYC requirements.
- To facilitate escrow transactions and dispute resolution.
- To provide personalized job recommendations.

## Data Retention
- KYC documents are retained for a minimum of 2 years after account closure.
- Transaction records are retained for 5 years for audit purposes.

## User Rights
- Users may request data deletion or export by contacting support.
- Users can update their personal information through the account settings.

## Security Measures
- Data at rest is encrypted using AES‑256.
- TLS 1.3 is enforced for all data in transit.
- Regular security audits and penetration testing are performed.

---
*This policy is a living document and will be updated to reflect regulatory changes.*
