# Privacy Policy

- **Effective date:** 13 November 2026
- **Version:** 1.1
- **Language:** English

> Implementation note: this published policy is aligned with the Yekis codebase and the Personal Data Protection Proclamation No. 1321/2024, but external Ethiopian legal counsel must approve the final production text before it is treated as counsel-approved.

Yekis is an Ethiopian freelance marketplace operated from Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. This Privacy Policy explains how Yekis collects, uses, shares, retains, and protects personal data when you visit the Yekis website, create an account, use the web workspace or Telegram Mini App, interact with the support team, post a job, bid on work, enter a contract, fund or receive a milestone payment, or use another Yekis feature.

Yekis is the data controller for personal data processed through those services. The marketplace parties also act as independent controllers for information they provide to one another for a transaction, such as a client's business contact details shown to the freelancer they hire.

## 1. Who we are and what this policy covers

This policy covers the Yekis website, browser workspace, Telegram Mini App and bot, support services, account registration, identity verification, jobs and proposals, contracts, milestone funding, wallet transactions, withdrawals, disputes, reviews, notifications, and communications with Yekis.

Yekis processes personal data to operate and secure the marketplace, perform the transactions requested by its users, comply with legal obligations, and improve the service. The data-subject rights described below apply to personal data linked to a Yekis account and to the extent applicable law provides them to other people whose data Yekis handles.

## 2. Information we collect

Yekis collects only information that is needed to operate, secure, and improve the marketplace.

### Account and authentication information

- First and last name, display name, email address, phone number, and date of birth.
- Account role, language and timezone preferences, and account creation or update times.
- Password hashes, session information, linked Google or other provider identifiers, and Telegram user or chat identifiers.
- Verification and account-security status.

### Profile and marketplace information

- Biography, avatar, skills, portfolio files, availability, and profile visibility.
- Job briefs, proposals, cover letters, screening answers, gig listings and packages.
- Contracts, milestone descriptions, delivery files, messages, reviews, profile views, and feature-placement activity.

### Identity and compliance information

- Verification tier, UCID, Fayda biometric identifier, identity documents, and a selfie or other verification image when you choose to submit one.
- Consent records, review decisions, transaction-monitoring flags, and suspicious transaction report records where the law requires them.

### Financial and payment information

- Wallet balance, ETB amounts, top-up and payment status, and provider transaction references.
- Withdrawals and payout destinations, ledger entries, platform fees, provider charges, statutory deductions, and financial audit events.

Yekis does not store complete payment-card credentials. Payment providers process those details directly.

### Communications

- Support conversations, direct messages, dispute statements and evidence, and notifications.
- Email, SMS, and Telegram delivery records.
- Consent choices.

### Technical and security information

- IP address, device and browser information, and request identifiers.
- Authentication and security events, timestamps, error records, and audit-log entries for important state changes.

## 3. Sensitive identity and financial information

Some information used by a freelance marketplace is sensitive. Identity documents, biometric identifiers, date of birth, financial records, and dispute evidence can create serious harm if exposed. Yekis processes this information only for the purpose for which you submit it, including identity verification, fraud prevention, payment execution, contract administration, dispute handling, and legal or regulatory compliance.

Do not place unnecessary sensitive information in a job brief, proposal, message, portfolio item, or dispute statement. Access to KYC documents, financial records, and other restricted records is limited by staff role and is written to the audit trail.

## 4. Why we use your information

Yekis uses personal data to:

- Create and secure your account, authenticate you, and recover access.
- Verify identity, screen for fraud, enforce marketplace rules, and comply with anti-money-laundering requirements.
- Publish the profile, portfolio, listings, proposals, reviews, and other content you choose to make visible to other users.
- Match clients and freelancers, create contracts, administer milestones, process payments, and calculate fees.
- Send service, delivery, payment, security, and account notifications you have asked to receive.
- Provide support, resolve disputes, enforce contracts, and maintain the marketplace record.
- Protect Yekis, its users, and the public from fraud, abuse, spam, and unlawful activity.
- Understand aggregate platform use and improve the product when you have permitted analytics.
- Meet tax, regulatory, audit, and other legal obligations.

## 5. Why we are permitted to process it

Depending on the processing activity, Yekis relies on one or more of the following legal bases under applicable law, including the Personal Data Protection Proclamation No. 1321/2024:

- **Performance of a contract:** processing needed to provide the service you request, such as accounts, contracts, milestones, and payouts.
- **Legal obligation:** identity, AML, tax, audit, or record-keeping law requiring Yekis to process or retain information.
- **Legitimate interests:** processing necessary for security, fraud prevention, service improvement, and marketplace integrity, after balancing those interests against your rights.
- **Consent:** optional analytics and marketing communications, and processing where the law specifically requires consent.

You may withdraw consent at any time through account settings or by contacting Yekis. Withdrawal does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal or processing based on another legal basis.

## 6. When we share information

Yekis does not sell personal data. Yekis shares information only for the purposes described in this policy, including:

- **Other marketplace users:** information needed to present your public profile, communicate with you, administer a proposal, contract, milestone, review, or dispute, and show identity information required for a transaction.
- **Service providers:** hosting, database, object storage, Redis, email, SMS, monitoring, and support providers that process data on Yekis's instructions.
- **Payment providers:** Telebirr and other providers configured for a transaction, to verify payment, hold or release milestone funds, disburse withdrawals, and reconcile records.
- **Identity and authentication providers:** eSignet or Fayda for verification, Google or another configured identity provider for sign-in, and Telegram for Mini App authentication and messages.
- **Analytics and service providers you permit:** Google Analytics is loaded only in a production configuration after you accept analytics cookies.
- **Professional advisers and authorities:** auditors, lawyers, regulators, courts, law-enforcement bodies, or financial-intelligence authorities when disclosure is required or necessary to protect the platform and its users.
- **A business transaction:** a merger, financing, restructuring, or sale, subject to confidentiality safeguards and continued notice to affected users.

## 7. Storage and international transfers

Yekis is designed to host its primary application, database, and object storage in Ethiopia. The current production deployment uses an Ethiopian cloud region. Access from another country, use of a global identity or communication provider, or support access from outside Ethiopia can nevertheless constitute an international transfer.

Where a transfer is necessary, Yekis uses the access, contractual, and technical safeguards required by applicable law. Contact Yekis for information about the providers and safeguards relevant to your data.

## 8. How we protect information

Yekis applies proportionate technical and organisational safeguards, including:

- Argon2id password hashing and short-lived, revocable session tokens.
- HTTPS for production traffic, security headers, a content security policy, rate limiting, and strict request validation.
- Role-based access to identity, compliance, financial, support, and dispute records.
- Restricted object storage and time-limited signed links for sensitive files.
- Append-only audit records for important financial, security, and administrative changes.
- Risk controls that flag unusual transaction patterns for human review.

No online service can promise absolute security. If a breach affects your information, Yekis will contain the incident, revoke affected sessions, notify affected users and the appropriate authority within the legally required period, and record the response.

## 9. How long we keep information

Yekis keeps personal data only as long as necessary for the purpose it was collected, to perform the service, to resolve disputes, and to meet legal obligations. The main retention periods are:

- **Active account and marketplace content:** while the account is active and as needed to operate the transactions and relationships entered into.
- **Identity, KYC, financial, transaction, consent, dispute, and audit records:** generally for up to ten years where applicable law or regulatory record-keeping rules require retention.
- **Support conversations and dispute evidence:** while the matter is open and for the period needed to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.
- **Security and technical records:** for the period needed to investigate incidents, enforce the terms, and protect the service.
- **Consent history:** retained as evidence of what you agreed to and when.

When you request erasure, Yekis destroys profile content, credentials, uploaded personal media, chat and direct-message content written by you, portfolio items, skills, marketplace copy, notifications, and analytics. The account is closed and its sessions are revoked.

Records that law requires Yekis to retain, including identity evidence, KYC records, wallets, transactions, ledger entries, audit logs, consent history, contracts, milestones, disputes, evidence, and reviews written about you, remain available for the required period. The erasure response lists the retained categories.

## 10. Cookies, browser storage, and analytics

Yekis uses browser storage for functions that cannot work without it, including authentication tokens, theme and language preferences, consent choices, and temporary payment-return state. Essential storage is not switched off because it is required for account security and the service to function.

Optional analytics uses Google Analytics in production only when a measurement identifier is configured and you have accepted analytics cookies. Yekis's analytics events are designed not to include email addresses, phone numbers, names, account identifiers, or user-specific payment amounts. You can reject analytics and marketing choices in the consent banner and change your mind later.

## 11. Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:

- Know what personal data Yekis holds about you and obtain a copy.
- Receive that data in a portable, machine-readable format.
- Correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
- Ask Yekis to erase personal data when no legal obligation requires retention.
- Withdraw consent and stop optional marketing or analytics processing.
- Restrict or object to processing that is based on legitimate interests.
- Receive notice of a legally required disclosure and challenge it where the law allows.
- Complain to the competent Ethiopian data protection authority.

Yekis cannot delete financial or identity records that must be retained by law, and cannot erase records while money is in flight, a contract is active, or a dispute is open. In those cases, the request is refused with the specific items that must first be resolved.

## 12. How to exercise your rights

Signed-in users can export their data and request account erasure from account settings. Export produces the account, profile, verification, financial, marketplace, communications, consent, and compliance records that Yekis holds. Erasure requires fresh authentication so that another person cannot close your account without your consent.

For a correction, restriction, objection, complaint, or any other privacy request, email `privacy@yekis.com`. Include enough detail for Yekis to identify the account and the request. Yekis may ask for verification before acting. Yekis aims to acknowledge a request promptly and will explain if a lawful exception applies.

## 13. Automated checks and human review

Yekis uses rules to detect unusual transaction patterns, possible duplicate or fraudulent accounts, incomplete verification, security anomalies, and content that may breach the terms. These rules can place an account, payment, or listing under review. Final compliance, KYC, and dispute decisions are made by authorised people through controlled workflows and are recorded in the audit trail.

You can ask for the information used to make a decision, request human review, or contest an outcome where applicable law gives you that right.

## 14. Children and minimum age

Yekis is not intended for children. You must be at least 18 years old and legally able to enter a binding contract to create an account or use the marketplace. Yekis does not knowingly collect personal data directly from a child. If you believe a child has provided information to Yekis, contact Yekis immediately so it can review and remove it where required.

## 15. Changes to this policy

Yekis may update this policy when the service, its processing, or the law changes. The effective date and version at the top of this document identify the current version. Yekis will provide additional notice through the service or by email when a change materially affects your rights. Continuing to use Yekis after the notice means the updated policy applies from its effective date, except where the law requires fresh consent.

## 16. Contact and complaints

Yekis is based in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. Send privacy questions to `privacy@yekis.com`. For general account or marketplace support, email `support@yekis.com`.

If you are not satisfied with the response, you may ask for escalation to Yekis's privacy contact or complain to the competent Ethiopian data protection authority. Yekis will not retaliate against you for making a good-faith privacy request or complaint.
