## The Problem

Every day in Ethiopia, clients and freelancers transact through DMs, Telegram groups, and verbal promises:

- **Clients fear** paying upfront and never receiving the work.
- **Freelancers fear** delivering work and never getting paid.
- **Nobody verifies** who is on the other side.

One side always has to *trust* — and when trust breaks, the money is already gone.

---

## What Yekis Does

Yekis sits in the middle of every transaction, holding the milestone payment while the work is done:

> **The client funds a milestone and the money stays held while the work is delivered and reviewed. The freelancer can see the milestone is funded before they start.**

1. **Verified identity** — every client and freelancer passes identity verification before transacting.
2. **Milestone funded** — the client funds a milestone; Yekis holds the amount.
3. **Work is delivered and reviewed** — the money moves when the client approves, or automatically after the review window if nobody responds.
4. **Fair dispute resolution** — if either party raises a dispute, the money stays held until a structured mediation process decides — not the loudest voice.
5. **Clean payouts** — approved earnings are settled to the freelancer's Telebirr or bank account.

**The platform is live and operational today** - milestone payment holding, verification, wallets, dispute process, and payouts, in a mobile-first experience built for the Ethiopian market.
